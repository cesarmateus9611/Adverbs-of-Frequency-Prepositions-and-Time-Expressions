Section 2 · Sentence Position

Where do they go?

The position of an adverb of frequency depends on the verb. This is the most important grammar rule!

Position Rules
1
Before the main verb (action verbs)
I always have breakfast. · She usually wakes up late. · They never study on Sundays.
2
After the verb to be
He is always tired. · We are often late. · I am never bored in class.
3
After auxiliary / modal verbs (can, do, does, will…)
She can sometimes help. · Do you often code at night?
4
Sometimes, usually, often, frequently can also go at the start or end
Sometimes I go to the gym. · I go to the gym sometimes.
⚠️
Never put a frequency adverb between the verb and its object
❌ I eat always breakfast. · ❌ She watches usually TV.
Section 3 · Prepositions of Time

in · on · at

These three prepositions tell us when something happens. Each one is used with specific types of time.

Prep.
Used with
in
months years seasons decades centuries long periods parts of day* in January · in 2026 · in summer · in the morning · in the afternoon · in the evening · in the 1990s
on
days of the week dates specific days holidays on Monday · on Friday evenings · on March 11th · on Christmas Day · on weekdays · on weekends
at
clock times precise moments meal times special periods at 7 a.m. · at noon · at midnight · at night · at breakfast · at the weekend (British) · at Christmas (period)

* Exception: at night (not "in night") · at noon / midnight

Section 4 · Frequency Expressions

every · once · twice · per

These expressions go at the end of the sentence (not before the verb like adverbs).

every dayI study every day.
every weekWe meet every week.
every monthThey pay every month.
every yearWe travel every year.
every morningShe runs every morning.
every other dayI exercise every other day.
once a weekI go to the gym once a week.
twice a monthWe have tests twice a month.
three times a yearI visit family three times a year.
four times a dayI check email four times a day.
once in a whileI watch movies once in a while.
from time to timeFrom time to time I cook.
every now and thenEvery now and then we hang out.
once a yearThey upgrade systems once a year.
twice a weekI attend coding club twice a week.
per day / per weekThree commits per day.
Position: once/twice/every → end of sentence
✅ I go to the gym twice a week.
❌ I twice a week go to the gym.
Section 5 · Questions

How to ask?

📌 How often…?
❓ How often do you exercise? → I exercise twice a week.
❓ How often does she check messages? → She always checks messages in the morning.
❓ How often do they go to the gym? → They go once a month.
❓ When do you study? → I study in the evening.
❓ What time do you wake up? → I wake up at 6 a.m.
